Digital identity, expressed in physical space.

01.06

Layout, radius and motion

ACCESS avoids generic card-dashboard composition. Spatial storytelling gets priority; admin UI exists to support control and auditability.

Grid

1320px max canvas

Fluid gutters, asymmetric 60/40 media-to-copy composition and full-bleed architectural sections.

Geometry

8–18px radius

Softened hardware and credential forms; avoid bubbly SaaS cards.

Motion

State-driven only

Reader pulse, validation sweep and permission transitions explain system state. Reduced-motion keeps state changes without loops.
