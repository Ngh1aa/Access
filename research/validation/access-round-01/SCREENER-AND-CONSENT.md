# ACCESS Round 01 — Screener + Consent

## Preferred participants

Prioritize people with recent experience in one or more of:
- workplace IT / identity administration;
- physical security / access control;
- facilities operations;
- visitor management;
- enterprise IT administration involving permissions or device/site status.

Adjacent enterprise administrators can participate as `PROXY`; do not represent them as physical-security practitioners.

## Screener

1. Have you managed employee/visitor access, identity permissions, sites/devices, or enterprise operational exceptions in the past 12 months?
2. Have you handled temporary access, revocation, offboarding or emergency access states?
3. Are you comfortable reviewing a fictional prototype for 30–40 minutes?
4. Are you willing to let anonymized behavioral observations be used in a portfolio case study? Optional.
5. Do you have accessibility/input needs that should be supported in the session?

## Do not collect

Do not ask participants for:
- real badge/credential identifiers;
- real office floor plans or restricted areas;
- employer access policies or emergency procedures;
- identity-provider credentials;
- customer/employee personal data;
- security incident details.

## Consent script

Confirm before the session:
- ACCESS is a portfolio prototype, not a deployed security system;
- hardware/NFC/BLE/SCIM and backend behavior are simulated;
- participation is voluntary and can stop at any time;
- notes use participant IDs, not names;
- recording requires separate explicit permission;
- portfolio use of anonymized evidence requires separate explicit permission;
- no employer-confidential information should be shared.

## Evidence classification

- Matching workplace IT / physical-security / facilities / identity practitioner → `DIRECT_USER` for this concept audience.
- Adjacent enterprise admin → `PROXY`.
- Designer/developer critique → peer review, not user-validation evidence.
