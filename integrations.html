CATEGORY 01

Identity & Single Sign-On (SSO)

Okta

SCIM v2.0
  • What syncs: Users, group memberships, suspended status, and MFA device status.
  • What it enables: Instant provisioning of Apple Wallet credentials upon group assignment; immediate door revocation upon suspension.
  • Why it matters: Eliminates orphan badges and ensures SOC 2 compliance.

Microsoft Entra ID (Azure AD)

Graph API
  • What syncs: Active Directory security groups, organizational units (OUs), and building location tags.
  • What it enables: Conditional physical access policies based on office assignments and security clearance tiers.
  • Why it matters: Full directory sync without installing local on-premise connector agents.

Google Cloud Identity

OAuth 2.0
  • What syncs: Google Workspace user directory and Google Calendar meeting invites.
  • What it enables: Automatic visitor pre-registration when a calendar invite includes an external email domain.
  • Why it matters: Zero extra steps for employees hosting client meetings.
CATEGORY 02

HRIS & People Operations

Workday

Enterprise API
  • What syncs: Employee hire dates, termination dates, manager hierarchies, and remote vs. onsite status.
  • What it enables: Day-one mobile badge issuance scheduled to activate automatically at 08:00 AM on the employee's start date.
  • Why it matters: Prevents HR from having to submit separate IT badge tickets.

BambooHR

Webhooks
  • What syncs: Employee records, office location changes, and contractor contract end dates.
  • What it enables: Auto-expiration of contractor passes the exact day contract agreements expire.
  • Why it matters: Closes the contractor security loophole automatically.
CATEGORY 03

Communications & Video Surveillance

Slack & Microsoft Teams

Bot Events
  • What syncs: Guest arrival events, door-held-open alerts, and lockdown confirmations.
  • What it enables: Host receives direct message with visitor photo and an interactive "Allow to Floor" button.
  • Why it matters: Hosts respond in seconds without leaving their primary work chat.

Axis & Verkada Video Systems

RTSP / ONVIF
  • What syncs: Camera stream feeds with access control unlock events.
  • What it enables: Automatic 10-second video clip bookmarking whenever an unauthorized card or forced door event occurs.
  • Why it matters: Instant visual context without scrubbing hours of separate security footage.
