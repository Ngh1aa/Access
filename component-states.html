02.04

Door and network recovery

Physical systems fail differently from web forms. Offline, jammed and degraded states need explicit safe behavior.

OFFLINE

Reader lost cloud connectivity

Show local-controller fallback policy, last-sync time and whether cached credentials remain valid.

DOOR HELD

Latch remained open

Raise an operational alert with door, site, duration and owner instead of only flashing red.

SYNC DELAY

Identity change pending

Display connector status and avoid promising instant revocation until the change is confirmed.

LOCKDOWN ERROR

One device did not acknowledge

Preserve global lockdown state while isolating the unconfirmed reader for manual security response.
