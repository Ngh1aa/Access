Chapter 02 · The Guest Experience

Security that feels welcoming.

Chapter 05 · Operational Resilience

Built for moments that matter.

Workplace security isn’t tested during standard routines — it’s proven when plans change unexpectedly. Explore how ACCESS handles edge scenarios automatically.

AUTOMATED ONBOARDING

Day one access ready before they step out of the car.

When an employee is created in Workday or Okta, ACCESS provisions an encrypted Apple Wallet credential straight to their welcome email. They tap through the front lobby speed gates on day one without waiting for HR or reception.

Eliminates 15+ minutes of reception badge creation per new hire.

What happens automatically:

Workday status toggles to Active
SCIM sync pushes profile to ACCESS instantly
Apple Wallet pass emailed and sent via SMS
Employee taps phone at entrance — access granted
PREMIUM VISITOR FLOW

A welcoming arrival without security compromises.

Client visits are pre-cleared with electronic NDA completion. When Marcus Chen arrives, scanning his pass automatically alerts host Đỗ Anh Nghĩa on Slack, calls the designated elevator, and grants access strictly to Floor 03.

Instant host ping & zero lobby paperwork.

What happens automatically:

Guest scans QR at lobby turnstile
Host notified via Slack that guest has arrived
Elevator preset to Floor 03 automatically
Meeting ends — pass deactivates automatically
TIME-BOUNDED PERMISSION

Strict zone boundaries that expire to the second.

Contractors and facility technicians receive passes strictly authorized for their specific work order (e.g. Server Room 4B) and active only during scheduled hours (13:00–15:00). Attempted entry anywhere else is immediately flagged.

Permission boundaries:

Authorized: Server Room 4B during work hours only
Blocked: Executive Floor 04 — access denied and logged
Auto-expiry: Pass revoked across all controllers
INSTANT ZERO-TRUST REVOCATION

Revoke in 1 second without rekeying hardware.

Employee misplaced their phone? IT or the employee can trigger instant remote kill via mobile portal or MDM. The cryptographic certificate is immediately invalidated across all local reader mesh nodes in < 500ms.

What happens instantly:

Revocation broadcast via encrypted cloud mesh
All 48 readers updated in under half a second
Replacement pass issued to backup device
DE-PROVISIONING SECURITY

Physical access revoked the exact minute digital access ends.

Traditional physical security suffers from forgotten plastic keycards. ACCESS binds physical credentials to Okta/Workday SSO status: when an account is disabled in Okta, all physical building doors seal instantly.

Audit trail guarantee:

Zero orphan badges circulating in the field
SOC 2 audit log generated automatically
Confirmed zero residual access across all sites
CRITICAL INCIDENT PROTOCOL

One-click site lockdown across all locations.

In the event of an active threat or environmental emergency, authorized administrators can trigger site-wide lockdown from their phone or browser. All perimeter turnstiles and doors secure immediately while preserving free egress per life safety codes.

Life Safety Compliance:

  • ✓ Exterior doors locked to all incoming credentials
  • ✓ Emergency push-bar egress remains 100% compliant
  • ✓ Local first responder master tokens remain operational
