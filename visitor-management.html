THE 7-STAGE GUEST JOURNEY

How modern workplaces welcome guests.

Zero friction for the guest, total auditability for security teams. Every step is tracked in real-time.

STAGE 01

Pre-Register

Host invites visitor via calendar invite. Guest receives a branded email link with parking instructions and building entry coordinates.

STAGE 02

Verify Identity

Optional government ID photo upload or biometric selfie verification for high-security commercial and financial facilities.

STAGE 03

Sign NDA

Sign non-disclosure agreements, safety protocols, and health waivers on phone or touchless lobby kiosk before arrival.

STAGE 04

Check In

Scan dynamic mobile QR code directly at the turnstile or tablet kiosk. No queues, no paper logs.

STAGE 05

Enter Approved Floor

Speed gate releases; elevator destination dispatch is automatically called to take the guest directly to Floor 03.

STAGE 06

Meet Host

Host Đỗ Anh Nghĩa receives a rich notification on Slack / Teams: "Marcus Chen has cleared Turnstile 02 and is en route."

STAGE 07

Exit & Expire

When the meeting concludes or the guest scans out at the lobby, permissions instantly deactivate across all readers.
